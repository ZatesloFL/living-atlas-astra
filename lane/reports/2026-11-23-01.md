  block-preview calculation; hard floors still cannot be approved away.

  details. Per-block consent clears when changing Places.

  absolute `top:1000px` placement; notes stay in flow with a bounded sticky

  notes appropriately. Phones retain inline expandable notes.

  adjacent related-place blocks become cards. No decoration reaches around
  Depth, Trust, Show or content gates to manufacture hidden entries.

  figures have explicit credits or an honest missing-credit label. The hero
  reserves its aspect ratio, and the Page address uses adequate dark ink.
- Rerenders anchor the visible block at its viewport position, or the nearest
  surviving neighbor when it is filtered out. Expanded transcripts/layer notes
  and keyboard focus survive. Closing the settings sheet returns focus to its
  opener. A compact bar stays compact during rerenders, avoiding an extra

## File 16: grant terms and recovery

Fresh seeded and newly hired answer/tour Docents use look grants with
`expires:null`. The helper filter treats a missing expiry as standing, matching
the existing permission check. Suggest-scope helpers still get seven days;
proposal lifetimes and owner approval remain unchanged.

Staff initialization runs the upgrade on load in Page and 3D. For an old,
already-initialized store it replaces known platform-seeded look grants, whether
still active or expired, using the existing `issue`/receipt path. Old grant and
receipt history stays intact; the replacement is receipted and the upgrade is
idempotent. It does not revive revoked helpers, revoked/denied/missing Place
attachments, owner overrides or higher-capability helpers. Receipt verification
failure prevents changes. Saved profiles, pins and history survive reload.

Staff now lists expired leased helpers explicitly, with their expiry and the
owner's existing **Staff → Add a helper** path for a replacement lease. It
withholds their action buttons. A Place with no grants gets a distinct message.
Revocation still removes a standing Docent and persists across reloads; an owner
can also revoke an expired helper. No richer management UI was introduced.

These are browser-local attached-helper grants. The deployed proxy's session
leases, public eligibility rule, scope checks, accounting and caps are unchanged.
No database schema or Edge Function change was needed.
