## Design choices worth comparing
Staff proposes through explicit grants and human decisions, using one simulated AI boundary that can later change provider without changing the UI or rules. Helpers attach to existing Places; visitors do not create rooms. The history proposals improve sample/source labeling rather than inventing verified history. Staff actions and approved Things have leases while receipts preserve the history.
