## Design choices worth comparing
Diagnostic bootstrap, Page and the 3D runtime are separate modules. Optional controls/labels can fail without killing the scene; a failed core scene/UI uses a readable form. Recovery keeps access ceilings rather than opening restricted content to make an error disappear.
