## Design choices worth comparing
- The old Safe mode pill meant the URL contained `?safe`, selecting L4 (no post/shadows). It was not evidence of a PowerVR failure; a separate normal-session level cannot be recovered from that screenshot. Diagnostics now states the active level and real reason. A guarded full-quality retry keeps the fallback ladder and caps its starting DPR.
- Preview as is temporary and read-only; profile changes exit it. Split is actual clipped world rendering, not screenshots or side-by-side cards. Lens diff is gray wire geometry/rings with a text explanation in Build.
- Fixed/approved/open styles are enforced independently from content permission. Original content remains unchanged by viewer styles and personal layers.
- Deep enables Expert previews, while Integrate remains an explicit choice. Integration remembers consent at the current effective soft limits; changed limits require a fresh block preview and hard limits always win.
- Merges target one named audience version and cannot silently raise its scores. All vouches, ratings and identities in layer examples are clearly simulated; historical material remains to verify.
