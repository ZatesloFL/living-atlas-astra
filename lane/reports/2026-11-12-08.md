## Design choices worth comparing
The film is a generated four-slide, silent sequence with an HTML video player and custom seek control; audio is explicitly separate device speech, never imitation of a historical person. The photo wall contains six geometric illustrations. The bus is conceptual, not a documented vehicle reconstruction. No archive or historical quotation is fabricated.
