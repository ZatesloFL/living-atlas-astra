## Design choices worth comparing
- The old “Safe mode · Diagnostics” pill was set by the URL’s `?safe` flag, which deliberately selected no post and no shadows. It did not indicate a PowerVR fallback. There is no separate diagnostic payload establishing the level of Nic’s normal session.
- Full-quality retry uses DPR at most 1.25 and validates actual buffers/first render, retaining HDR → byte bloom → no post → no shadows recovery. No GPU blacklist was invented.
- Two fictional rooms demonstrate listed/hidden access while the family doorway stays boundary-only. No family interior, private people or home address was added.
- Access is local, labeled simulation. An invite link must be opened in the same browser with the recipient profile; it is not a cross-device permission service.
