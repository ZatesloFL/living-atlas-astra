## Design choices worth comparing

- Sign readability is independent of camera distance and adaptive 3D DPR. The nameplates still anchor to world positions, face the camera, and follow lens visibility. Foreground foliage cannot hide their text.
- Eye height rises to 1.88m, default pitch points slightly up, the portrait FOV is 67°, and the entrance is 2.2m closer to the courtyard. The title treatment is more compact to leave room for the world.
- Atlas label layout checks every marker’s clearance and other label boxes, instead of relying on a single fixed offset. Backplates keep labels readable where graph edges cross.
- Requested preferences are preserved rather than silently discarded when unverified. Applied limits are derived centrally and the HUD explicitly names the ceiling.
- Seeded Me is verified as required by v4, but visibly simulated. Normal first-run profiles have no backing. Display names and the verified backing never imply one another.
- Long-press uses a lift-then-tap wheel with six profiles per page. The quick chip is the simpler alternative for switching or managing many profiles.
- Profile switches use an append-only SHA-256 chain now because A4 explicitly requires receipts; room-access ledger behavior remains Tier B.

## Known problems

- Phone readability, composition, thumb comfort, long-press feel and hardware fps remain unconfirmed. Browser screenshots and touch-event tests cannot settle the physical Pixel experience.
- A nameplate may be omitted when it is outside the view or all nearby placements would overlap; the nearest visible signs have priority. Labels are navigation annotations drawn over foliage, so their text can remain visible when the physical object is partially occluded.
- Preferred Narrate/Text, style, trust/depth and layer choices are stored for future functionality where applicable; they are not presented as implemented narration, restyling, or layer integration.
- Storage blocked by the browser produces a session-only warning; persistence cannot be promised in that case. Corrupt saved receipt chains are preserved and block further receipt-writing operations rather than silently inventing a valid history. A fresh browser context can still start normally.
- Unsaved developer overrides intentionally disappear on reload; a saved test profile intentionally retains the TEST MODE banner on return. This is not real verification or authorization.
- This cloud environment still denies outbound access to the hosted Pages hostname and GitHub API. Git pushes are confirmed, but this session has not independently checked the newest hosted deployment response. The phone uses its own network.
- Tier B/C and the v3 features explicitly deferred by v4 are not implemented.
