## What changed and why

The earlier G3 implementation (report 2026-11-12-08) already supplied all five
components and passed its five original functional checks. This audit found
holes those checks did not cover:

- Newspaper “zoom” only widened the columns; glyphs stayed the same size. It now
  enlarges the whole clipping at 140%/180%, with horizontal panning and a visible
  selected zoom control. Columns keep a readable measure. The dark 3D sheet's
  paragraph color no longer overrides the parchment's dark ink or video captions.
- The 3D atlas had fixed photo indices, one shared film tile and a hardcoded
  newspaper headline. Each displayed Thing now gets its own tile from its
  settings, including independent film cues. Rebuilding a scene preserves the
  current cue. The eight boycott tiles still use one 1024×512 atlas and one draw.
- Component facing was ignored, and playback distance ignored room rotation and
  scaling. One placement helper now composes the component and Place transforms
  for geometry, collision bounds, labels and media distance.
- Page SVG and canvas art disagreed for several scene IDs. Both now use the same
  six procedural illustrations: canopy road, bus stop, walking group, community
  table, records and imagined skyline. No downloaded assets or archival claims.
- Replaced reader nodes could retain video views, and capture tracks remained
  alive after leaving. Detached views are pruned and tracks stop when no reader
  needs them or the visitor leaves. Native media events check current element
  state, so an old pause event cannot toggle a new player. A finished film resets
  its first caption before replay. Changing the Lens requires a fresh play tap.
- Gallery lightboxes now use the selected Thing's actual source pointer and close
  when their originating view disappears or loses access. Seat and zoom controls
  expose their selected state. Existing sample/verification captions remain.
- The Inside approach changes from [7, -3.3] to [9.5, -3.4], between displays.
  The old position put the portrait camera close to the film screen. The same
  data-driven travel gates and receipts still govern arrival.

No content or evidence rules were weakened. The authored blocks, component
schemas/behaviors and AI catalog revision are unchanged. This is the existing
bounded kit, with no editor, live archive connection or new room creation.
