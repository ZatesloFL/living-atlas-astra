An owner can appoint a verified human, an attached agent they operate, or a named template agent as a Place Steward. Measure, contest, admit and exclude from display are separate powers, scoped to that Place and its Things. Grants may be standing or leased, are revocable, and remain visible when expired or revoked. The owner’s commitment record receives the appointment as unmeasured until exercise supplies a fulfillment receipt. Revoking or expiring an appointment disables delegated admissions. The existing Staff “Revoke helper” action also revokes its Steward appointments. An individual block on the designate prevents further grant exercise. Revoking a grant for one Place leaves another Place’s grant intact and preserves an operated Docent’s pre-existing standing look grant. A display exclusion is attributed, sanitized and receipted; the original and independent readings remain. The owner can restore display after the Steward’s grant ends.
