# Standing derivation v1

File 26's four facets are records, never a points balance or an overall number. The implementation is `src/standing-facets.js`. Anyone can recompute it from the same immutable receipts and evaluation time.

Only acts on published subjects contribute. Private declarations, private measurement outcomes, visits and Lens settings never enter a public facet. Anonymous presentation carries no record. File 28 refines identity into account assurance and presentation; unverified accounts cannot file measurements. Authored sample `measured` fields are compatibility floors, not measurement receipts. Local identity and local acts remain labeled simulations; the proxy validation snapshot is real minimal server evidence with no visitor text or identity.

- **Declaration record:** group Steward readings by the exact declaration receipt they checked. Each declaration has one current conclusion: contested while a current reading awaits review; gap if any active independent Steward reading differs on either dial; otherwise held. A new outcome supersedes by receipt; the earlier receipts stay in full history. Places and Things have their own declaration records. Owner and Creator records resolve separately by their stable IDs. A seed declaration with no signed owner ID can contribute to its Place's record after measurement, but never to an invented person's record.
- **Measurement record:** a Steward's reading is unmeasured until another Steward re-measures it, or contested while awaiting that review. Matching re-measurement is held; a changed reading is a gap. The outcome links both receipts. An owner reading cannot suppress an independent one; a scoped contest outcome is the only cross-actor supersession.
- **Helper record:** use actual proxy validation receipts (held for accepted/validated, gap for rejected) plus receipted owner acceptance of a proposal and completed helper tours. Accepting a tour offer alone is not completion. Local scripted answers and network fallbacks do not become validated AI answers. Duplicate proxy UUIDs count once. The historical snapshot includes failures as well as successes and says when it was read; later responses received by this browser add their receipt IDs. There is no claim of continuous server synchronization.
- **Commitment record:** use observed fulfillment receipts, such as honoring a declared threshold/grant on an actual crossing and honoring a Steward appointment when it is exercised. Issuing or revoking a grant alone does not assert that a promise was kept or broken. No breach is inferred without an outcome receipt.

Each facet's current **reading** uses exponential recency with a 30-day half-life. Sum weights separately for held, gap and contested; the largest category supplies the word. Ties prefer contested, then gap, then held. Unmeasured entries remain in full history but cannot vote a result into existence. No relevant checked outcome means **unmeasured**, not zero and not untrusted. Numeric weights are internal to the published derivation and are never displayed or combined across facets. The full record remains one tap away. Receipts superseded by a repair remain visible in measurement history; no original receipt is edited or deleted.

For named or pseudonymous presentation, doors and Lenses consume these same words regardless of the holder’s browsing preference. Anonymous presentation supplies unmeasured readings and no record with a knock; sealed account accountability is future Second Look machinery, not a claim made by this local store. A holder may expose their full public person-level record or share its reading only at a knock, door or helper offer. Public measurement records and published Place declaration records remain public. Visiting history, private-room material and Lens settings stay in the private access/profile stores. Public audience records say that eligibility was checked; their ticket-stub receipt remains private.

The implementation is local to this browser, with hash-linked receipts and tamper detection. It does not claim remote identity verification, server signatures, synchronization or recovery from cleared site data. Projection reset does not erase the separate access/standing records. A later shared service must preserve these boundaries and the same derivation.


Standing rules are explicit owner terms: each names a facet, held or no gap/contest, its audience, its admission effect, and what to do with unmeasured. Every required rule must pass. An automatic admission supplies an enter grant only; an individual block, another required rule, a containing Place or a content hard limit still prevents entry. No authored public Place or starter ships a standing requirement. A tampered standing ledger disables crossings and writes.

The private Lens may warn or filter by a Place's declaration gap or contest. Unmeasured produces no warning. Audience readings affect content limits only when the holder opts in and no Steward has measured that subject; a Steward reading always takes precedence, and existing safety floors still bind.

Place Steward grants name the owner, designate, operator (for an agent), exact Place, powers and optional expiry. Measurement, contest, admission and exclusion from display are separate powers. Revocation and expiry disable exercise and every admission delegated through that grant. Prior measurements remain attributed evidence. A display exclusion changes presentation only, keeps the original and independent readings, names the chooser, and can be restored by the owner after revocation. Template agents record the operator's entered inspection; no autonomous or model inspection is claimed.
