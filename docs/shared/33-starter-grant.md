# 33 — The starter grant: the Atlas starts hooked up

Ruled by Nic, 2026-11-26, from Duet's designer playtests (runs 1 and 2):
the first thing a newcomer met was a refusal for want of a grant, and the
way through was a constitutional sentence hidden in an example. Nic's
ruling, in his words: the Atlas "should basically be hooked up to do
everything" — full starter grant, ask-at-the-edges.

## The ruling

At arrival, the holder's grant to the Atlas **already exists**: edit scope
across Lens, Travel, Discovery, Build, Sources, and standing — everything
a person's own grant can cover. There is no sentence to find and no
proposal to approve before the Atlas can help.

- **Told in one line, labeled honestly.** Arrival presents it in one plain
  sentence ("Atlas can help with everything here from the start — narrow
  or turn off any of it anytime, in words"). The record says what it is:
  a **starter grant, given at arrival**. It is never presented as a
  confirmed proposal or as a choice the holder made — the Atlas never
  declares what the holder never declared.
- **Scope is not initiative.** The starter grant is reach, not license to
  act unasked. The initiative envelope still starts at **Conversational**:
  the Atlas proposes and acts on the holder's word. **Proactive** — acting
  or watching unasked — begins only when the holder asks for it in words,
  with its envelope specified, as file 31 already rules. Attending still
  starts off and is visibly off.
- **Inside is free; the edges ask.** Within the holder's own places and
  settings, the Atlas acts on their word with no further asks. An action
  that reaches **outside** asks first, in plain words, at the moment it
  arises — never as an up-front battery:
  - publishing or changing anything so **others can see or enter** it;
  - **connecting an external source**;
  - **appointing another helper or steward**, or granting anything to
    another person;
  - anything that **touches another person** (invites, messages, shared
    access);
  - anything that **costs** something.
  This is the owner's standing rule from his own products: we don't
  probe, we ask when relevant.
- **Floors and ceilings untouched.** Hard floors cannot be granted away —
  the starter grant changes nothing there. The holder's Lens and their
  account's assurance ceilings bind exactly as before: an unverified
  account's adult doors stay closed, grant or no grant.
- **Narrowing is forever one sentence away.** Narrow, suspend, or revoke
  in words at any time; effective immediately and receipted. The sentence
  that used to be the way in ("Give Atlas standing edit access…") remains
  valid as the way to **restore** full scope after narrowing.
- **Existing choices stand.** A profile that already made any grant
  choice — narrowed, scoped, revoked — keeps it exactly. Only a profile
  with **no grant record at all** receives the starter, at its next
  entry, labeled a starter.

## What this supersedes

The no-default-grant term of the file 31 build contract (lane A report
09): "no default grant, posture, or Proactive watch; nothing applied
before a confirmed proposal." That term is replaced by this file for
**first access**. The proposal machinery itself stands and now serves
two things: **changes** to a holder's grant, and **edge asks** under this
file. File 31's contract otherwise stands entire — agents propose, rules
validate, receipts record; the Atlas acts only under the holder's grant;
nothing is declared in the holder's name that they did not confirm (the
starter is declared as the system's starting term, in the system's name,
which is the honest form).

## Acceptance shape

A fresh profile's first plain request ("keep it gentle, I'm here for
birds and bread") is answered, not refused. The grant record for that
profile reads as a starter given at arrival. The first edge action —
publishing to a place others can enter — asks, in plain words, before it
happens. A narrowed profile is never silently re-widened.
