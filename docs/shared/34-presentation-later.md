# 34 — Presentation comes later (owner ruling, 2026-11-28)

File 28 stands: the account is always real, and presentation — Named, Pseudonymous, or Anonymous presentation — is the person's choice over that one account. What this brief changes is **when the choice is asked: not at the door.**

## The ruling

1. **No presentation question at sign-in or onboarding.** Arrival never asks how the holder wants to be seen. The question is removed from the sign-in and profile-creation flow entirely — not deferred a step, not collapsed into a default with a prompt behind it.
2. **Everyone starts Named.** A new profile's presentation is Named, recorded honestly as the starting presentation — the same posture as the starter grant (brief 33): the starting state is told in plain words where the holder can see it, never dressed as a choice they made.
3. **The choice lives in the profile.** Presentation can be changed at any time from the profile, in words, effective immediately and receipted, like any other setting. Pseudonymous means choosing the persistent name the world knows; Anonymous presentation means walking as a visitor, exactly as file 28 §4 defines them. One account, one public identity — unchanged.
4. **The Atlas may offer the choice in context, once, where it first matters** — the holder's first publication, or their first knock at a place where they would be seen by others. An offer, in plain words ("This will show your name — keep it, use a different name, or show no name"), never a gate: publishing and knocking proceed as Named if the holder ignores it. After that first offer, the Atlas does not raise it again; the profile setting is the way.
5. **Authorship follows presentation.** How a holder's name attaches to the things they build and publish is their presentation at the time — Named shows their name, Pseudonymous shows the chosen name, Anonymous presentation shows no name. Changing presentation later does not rewrite the authorship already recorded on published things; receipts keep what was true when they were written.

## Why

The account layer is real from the first minute, so the sign-in question asked newcomers to decide how the world sees them before they had seen the world — a probe, not a need. The Atlas's standing rule is to ask when relevant (brief 31; brief 33's ask-at-the-edges). Presentation becomes relevant at the first public act, and not before.

## Scope

- Applies to the prototype's TEST MODE profile creation exactly as to real accounts: the local stand-in flow drops the question too, and its profiles start Named.
- Assurance (§5 of file 28), guardianship, and the hard floors are untouched. Anonymous presentation remains privacy from the world, never from the floors.
- Nothing here re-opens file 28's scrapping of account-less entry: no account, no entry.
